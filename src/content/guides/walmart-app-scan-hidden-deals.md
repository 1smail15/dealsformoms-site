That sign above isn't unusual — I see some version of it on most trips now.
**"Shop all the Deals in the app"**, printed right on the endcap, next to a
price that's only good if you actually open the app. The shelf tag alone
doesn't tell you everything.

## What this actually means

Walmart runs app-exclusive and Walmart+-exclusive pricing on top of regular
clearance markdowns. The $17.88 on that sign is the walk-up price — members
with early access, or anyone who scans the item in the app, can sometimes
see a different number. The sticker on the box is a starting point, not the
final word.

## How to actually use it in store

1. **Open the app before you shop**, not after you've filled the cart —
   some early-access windows are time-limited.
2. **Scan the barcode**, not just the price tag. The app pulls the current
   system price, which is what actually rings up at register — it can be
   lower (or occasionally higher) than the shelf tag.
3. **Check for a QR code on the endcap itself**, like the one in the photo
   above — it usually links straight to that specific promotion instead of
   a generic search.
4. **If you're a Walmart+ member, look for "early access"** language on the
   signage — that's telling you the price you see posted isn't the price
   everyone gets yet.

## The takeaway

Treat the printed shelf price as a floor estimate, not the answer. If a
sign is specifically calling out the app, that's Walmart telling you there's
a better number one scan away.

*Prices and stock are per store and change daily. Scan before you drive.*
