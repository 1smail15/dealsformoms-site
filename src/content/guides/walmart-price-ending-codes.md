If you have ever wondered why one clearance tag says `$8.00` and the one next to
it says `$8.03`, you are looking at the most useful signal on the shelf.

## The short version

Walmart's system marks items down in tiers, and the cents digits are how staff
track where an item sits in that sequence. Two endings matter most:

- **Prices ending in `.00`** — a rollback or a planned promotional price. It can
  still fall further.
- **Prices ending in `.03`** — in most stores this signals a final markdown. What
  you see is the floor; the next step is the item leaving the shelf entirely.

## Why this is worth knowing

The difference decides whether you buy now or wait a week. A `.00` tag on
seasonal stock in early September will usually be cheaper by the end of the
month. A `.03` tag will not — and it is far more likely to be gone.

> One caveat worth stating plainly: this is a convention, not a published policy,
> and it varies by store and by district. Treat it as a strong hint, not a rule.

## What to do at the shelf

1. Check the cents digits before anything else.
2. Scan the item in the Walmart app — the shelf tag is often behind the system price.
3. If it reads `.03` and the app price is lower still, buy it.
